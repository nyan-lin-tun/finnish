## Grammar

- **Minä olen…** — I am… You can also say **Olen…**
- **Minulla on…** — I have… Use this to describe your family.
- **Pidän… / En pidä…** — I like… / I do not like… Use the **-sta/-stä** form with this meaning of *pitää*: **kahvi → kahvista**, **pasta → pastasta**, **pizza → pizzasta**.
- **Sinä** is singular “you”; **te** is plural “you” and can also be used for polite address.

## Mistakes to remember

- **avain**, not *avai* — key.
- **ikkuna**, not *ikuna* — window.
- **Eilen oli sunnuntai.** Use **oli** (“was”) for the past; do not add **on** (“is”).
- Languages and weekdays use lowercase: **englantia**, **suomea**, **maanantai**.

## Practice

1. Introduce yourself and say your first name and surname.
2. Name three classroom objects using **Tämä on…**
3. Say what day it is today, yesterday, and tomorrow.
4. Name three things you like using **Pidän…**
5. Replace the example time and dislikes with your own answers.
