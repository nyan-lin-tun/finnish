## Weekdays — Viikonpäivät

Finnish weekday names use lowercase. Use the **-na/-nä** form to say **on** a particular weekday.

| English | Finnish | On that day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | maanantai | maanantaina |
| Tuesday | tiistai | tiistaina |
| Wednesday | keskiviikko | keskiviikkona |
| Thursday | torstai | torstaina |
| Friday | perjantai | perjantaina |
| Saturday | lauantai | lauantaina |
| Sunday | sunnuntai | sunnuntaina |

## When? — Milloin?

**Milloin sinä yleensä menet kauppaan?**  
When do you usually go to the shop?

**Yleensä menen kauppaan lauantaina ja sunnuntaina.**  
I usually go to the shop on Saturday and Sunday.

- **yleensä** — usually
- **kauppaan** — to the shop
- **Milloin?** asks “When?”; **Mikä päivä?** asks “What day?”

## Yesterday, today, and tomorrow

This example follows the PDF: **today is Friday**. It is a practice example, not a statement about the current day.

| English | Finnish | Example | Translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The day before yesterday | toissapäivänä | Toissapäivänä oli keskiviikko. | The day before yesterday was Wednesday. |
| Yesterday | eilen | Eilen oli torstai. | Yesterday was Thursday. |
| Today | tänään | Tänään on perjantai. | Today is Friday. |
| Tomorrow | huomenna | Huomenna on lauantai. | Tomorrow is Saturday. |
| The day after tomorrow | ylihuomenna | Ylihuomenna on sunnuntai. | The day after tomorrow is Sunday. |

**Oli** means “was”; **on** means “is”. Here, Finnish also uses **on** for future days; the time word makes the meaning clear.

## Weather — Sää

**Millainen sää on?** — What is the weather like?

| English | Finnish |
| --- | --- |
| The sun is shining. | Aurinko paistaa. |
| It is cloudy. | On pilvistä. |
| It is snowing. | Sataa lunta. |
| It is windy. | Tuulee. |
| It is raining. | Sataa vettä. |
| It is hot. | On kuuma. |
| It is cold. | On kylmä. |
| There is a thunderstorm. | On ukkonen. |
| It is foggy. | On sumuista. |
| It is partly cloudy. | On puolipilvistä. |
| It is warm. | On lämmintä. |

- **sumu** — fog
- **lämmin** — warm; **lämmintä** is the form used in **On lämmintä.**

### Here and there

**Täällä aurinko paistaa ja on kylmä.**  
Here the sun is shining and it is cold.

**Millainen sää siellä on?**  
What is the weather like there?

**Täällä on puolipilvistä.**  
It is partly cloudy here.

- **täällä** — here
- **siellä** — there

## Mistakes to remember

- Say **Aurinko paistaa**, not *Aurinko on paistaa*: **paistaa** is already the verb.
- **Tuulee** and **sataa** also work without **on**.
- Write **pilvistä**, **kylmä**, and **täällä**, keeping the Finnish letters.
- **Toissapäivänä** is written as one word.
- **Loppuu** is a verb meaning “ends / finishes”; **loppu** is the noun “end”.
